ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Connecting People With Modern Technology

Shamirah Electronics Company is a Ugandan electronics business focused on making modern technology easier to discover, understand and access.

The company provides a wide range of electronics categories including smartphones, laptops, televisions, appliances, audio equipment and accessories.

In addition to electronics, Shamirah provides supporting services such as installation, maintenance, repair, technical support and delivery arrangements.

Our slogan:
"Smart Technology. Simple Life."
